Subject: Starting March 1: Invoice approvals will escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi,

Starting March 1, invoice approval reminders will escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours by default. If the assigned approver hasn't acted within that window, the backup approver will be notified so the invoice can keep moving.

**Why we're making this change**

Many of you told us that invoices sit unapproved while approvers are out of office, on leave, or traveling. Default escalation means one person's absence no longer holds up payment.

**What you can do**

Escalation is on by default, and you can adjust it at any time in **Settings > Approvals > Escalation**:

- **Change the window** if 48 hours is too short or too long for your team.
- **Turn escalation off** if you prefer to keep approvals with the assigned approver only.

Before March 1, we recommend reviewing your backup approvers in Settings so escalations reach the right people.
