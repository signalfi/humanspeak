Subject: From March 1, unapproved invoices go to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi [First name],

Starting March 1, if nobody acts on an invoice approval reminder within 48 hours, Ledgerline will send the invoice to a backup approver. This will be on by default for every account.

You can make the window longer or shorter, or turn escalation off completely, in Settings > Approvals > Escalation.

We're making this change because customers told us invoices were sitting unapproved while approvers were out of the office. Until now, a reminder to someone on leave just sat in their inbox, and the invoice waited for them to come back.
