Subject: Starting March 1, invoice approvals will escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi,

Starting March 1, if an invoice approval reminder goes unanswered for 48 hours, Ledgerline will escalate it to a backup approver by default.

**Why we're making this change**
Many of you told us that invoices sit unapproved while approvers are out of the office. Automatic escalation keeps approvals moving when someone is unavailable.

**What you can adjust**
You can change the escalation window or turn escalation off entirely:

Settings > Approvals > Escalation

If the 48-hour default works for your team, you don't need to do anything.
