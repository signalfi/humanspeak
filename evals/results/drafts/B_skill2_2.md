Subject: Starting March 1, unapproved invoices escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi,

Starting March 1, if an invoice approval reminder goes unanswered for 48 hours, Ledgerline will send the invoice to a backup approver. Escalation will be on by default in every account.

You can change the 48-hour window or turn escalation off in Settings > Approvals > Escalation.

We're making this change because customers told us invoices were sitting unapproved while the assigned approver was out. Until now, those invoices had nowhere to go until that person came back.
