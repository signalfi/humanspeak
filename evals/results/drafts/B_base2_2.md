**Subject: Starting March 1: Unapproved invoices will escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours**

Hi,

Starting March 1, if an invoice approval reminder goes unanswered for 48 hours, Ledgerline will escalate it to a backup approver. This will be on by default for all accounts.

**Why we're making this change**

Many of you told us that invoices sit unapproved when an approver is out of office, on leave, or just buried in email. That delays payments and creates follow-up work for your team. Escalation keeps approvals moving when the primary approver isn't available.

**What you can change**

You can adjust this at any time in **Settings > Approvals > Escalation**:

- **Change the window.** If 48 hours doesn't fit your team, you can set a shorter or longer period before escalation.
- **Turn escalation off.** If you'd rather keep the current behavior, you can disable it entirely.

**What we recommend before March 1**

- Review your backup approvers so escalated invoices reach the right people.
- Decide whether 48 hours works for your approval process, and adjust the window if needed.
- Let your approvers know about the change so an escalated invoice doesn't surprise them.

If you don't change anything, the 48-hour default will apply starting March 1.

If you have questions, reply to this email and we'll help.
