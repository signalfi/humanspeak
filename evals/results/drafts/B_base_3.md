**Subject: Starting March 1: Invoice approvals will escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours**

Hi,

Starting March 1, Ledgerline will escalate invoice approval reminders to a backup approver when an invoice has gone unapproved for 48 hours. This will be on by default for all accounts.

**Why we're making this change**

Many of you told us that invoices sit unapproved when an approver is out of office, on leave, or busy. Payments get delayed, and someone ends up chasing down approvals by hand. Automatic escalation keeps invoices moving when the primary approver isn't available.

**What you can change**

You control how escalation works for your team. In **Settings > Approvals > Escalation**, you can:

- **Change the escalation window** if 48 hours is too short or too long for your team
- **Turn escalation off** if you'd rather keep your current approval flow

If the default works for you, you don't need to do anything.

**Before March 1**

We recommend reviewing your approval settings before the change takes effect, so escalations reach the right people.
