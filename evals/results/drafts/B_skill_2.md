Subject: Starting March 1, unapproved invoices go to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi,

Starting March 1, an invoice approval reminder that sits unanswered for 48 hours will be sent to that approver's backup. This is on by default for every Ledgerline account.

We're making this change because many of you told us invoices stall when the only person who can approve them is out. The invoice waits, the reminder waits in an inbox nobody is reading, and the vendor ends up following up with you.

If 48 hours doesn't suit your team, you can shorten or lengthen the window in Settings > Approvals > Escalation. You can also turn escalation off there. [NEED: whether a changed setting applies to invoices already waiting for approval or only to new ones]
