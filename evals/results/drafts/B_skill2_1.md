Subject: Starting March 1, unapproved invoices escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi [First name],

Starting March 1, if an invoice approval reminder goes unanswered for 48 hours, Ledgerline will send the invoice to a backup approver. This will be on by default for every account. You can change the 48-hour window or turn escalation off in Settings > Approvals > Escalation.

We're doing this because customers told us that invoices sit unapproved while approvers are out. Sending more reminders doesn't help when the approver is on leave and not reading email.

Before March 1, check who your backup approvers are, since they'll start receiving these invoices. [NEED: where admins assign backup approvers, and what happens to an invoice that has no backup approver.]
