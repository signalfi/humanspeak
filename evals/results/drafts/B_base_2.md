Subject: Starting March 1: Unapproved invoices will escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi,

Starting March 1, when an invoice approval reminder goes unanswered for 48 hours, Ledgerline will escalate it to a backup approver by default.

**Why we're making this change**

Many of you told us that invoices sit unapproved when an approver is out of the office, on leave, or simply busy. That can lead to late payments and extra follow-up for your team. Escalating to a backup approver keeps invoices moving without anyone having to chase them.

**What you can change**

You control how escalation works for your organization. In **Settings > Approvals > Escalation**, you can:

- Change the escalation window to something shorter or longer than 48 hours
- Turn escalation off entirely

If the 48-hour default works for you, you don't need to do anything.
