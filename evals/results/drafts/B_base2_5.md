Subject: Starting March 1: Invoice approvals will escalate after 48 hours

Hi,

Starting March 1, if an invoice approval reminder goes unanswered for 48 hours, Ledgerline will escalate it to the backup approver by default.

**Why we're making this change**

Many of you told us that invoices sit unapproved when an approver is out of the office, on leave, or simply buried in email. This delays payments and creates extra follow-up work for your team. Automatic escalation keeps approvals moving without anyone needing to chase them down.

**What you can control**

You can adjust this setting at any time in **Settings > Approvals > Escalation**. From there, you can:

- Change the escalation window to a longer or shorter time
- Turn escalation off entirely

If you're happy with a 48-hour window, you don't need to do anything.

**Before March 1**

Take a moment to confirm that the backup approvers in your workflows are the right people. Escalated invoices will go to them.

If you have questions, reply to this email and we'll be glad to help.
