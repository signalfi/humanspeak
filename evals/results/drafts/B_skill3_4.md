Subject: From March 1, unapproved invoices go to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi [First name],

Starting March 1, if an invoice approval reminder goes unanswered for 48 hours, Ledgerline will send the invoice to a backup approver. Escalation will be on by default for every account.

We made this change because customers told us invoices were sitting unapproved while the approver was out of the office. The invoice waited until that person came back. With escalation on, someone else gets the reminder once the window passes.

If 48 hours doesn't suit how your team works, you can set a different window or turn escalation off in Settings > Approvals > Escalation.
