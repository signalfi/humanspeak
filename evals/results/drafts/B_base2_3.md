Subject: Starting March 1: Unapproved invoices will escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi,

Starting March 1, if an invoice is still waiting for approval 48 hours after the first reminder, Ledgerline will send the approval request to a backup approver. This will be on by default for all accounts.

**Why we're making this change**

Many of you told us that invoices sit unapproved when an approver is out of the office, which can delay payments and put vendor relationships at risk. Escalation keeps invoices moving even when the original approver isn't available.

**What you can change**

You can adjust this in **Settings > Approvals > Escalation**:

- **Change the escalation window** if 48 hours is too short or too long for your team.
- **Turn escalation off** if you'd rather keep approvals with the original approver.

If the default works for you, you don't need to do anything.

**Before March 1**

We recommend checking that your backup approvers are set up the way you want, so escalated invoices reach the right people.

If you have questions, reply to this email and our team will help.
