Subject: From March 1, invoices waiting 48 hours will go to a backup approver

Hi,

Starting March 1, if an approver doesn't act on an invoice approval reminder within 48 hours, Ledgerline will send the invoice to a backup approver. This will be on by default for every account.

We're making this change because many of you told us that invoices sit unapproved while approvers are out. When someone is on vacation or off sick, their invoices often wait until they return. With escalation, the invoice goes to someone else without anyone first having to notice it's stuck.

You can make the 48-hour window shorter or longer, or turn escalation off completely, in Settings > Approvals > Escalation. If your team already has its own way of covering absences, you can switch it off there.
