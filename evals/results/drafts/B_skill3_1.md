Subject: From March 1, unapproved invoices go to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi [First name],

From March 1, if nobody acts on an invoice approval reminder within 48 hours, Ledgerline will escalate it to a backup approver. This will be on by default for every account.

To use a different window or turn escalation off, go to Settings > Approvals > Escalation.

We're making this change because customers told us invoices were sitting unapproved while approvers were out, and nothing moved until they got back.
