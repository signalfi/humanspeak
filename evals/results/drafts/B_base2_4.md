**Subject: Starting March 1, overdue invoice approvals will escalate to a backup approver**

Hi,

Starting March 1, Ledgerline will escalate invoice approval reminders to a backup approver if an invoice goes unapproved for 48 hours. This will be on by default for all accounts.

**Why we're making this change**

Many of you told us that invoices sit unapproved while approvers are out of office or on leave, which can delay payments and strain vendor relationships. Escalation keeps invoices moving when the primary approver isn't available.

**How it works**

- An approval request goes to the primary approver, as it does today.
- If the invoice is still unapproved after 48 hours, the reminder escalates to the backup approver.

**What you can adjust**

In **Settings > Approvals > Escalation**, you can:

- Change the escalation window to something shorter or longer than 48 hours
- Turn escalation off entirely

If the default works for your team, you don't need to do anything. Before March 1, we suggest confirming that the right people are set as backup approvers so escalated reminders reach someone who can act on them.
