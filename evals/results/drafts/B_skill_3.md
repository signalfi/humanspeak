Subject: From March 1, stalled invoice approvals will go to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi,

Starting March 1, if an invoice approval reminder goes unanswered for 48 hours, Ledgerline will escalate it to a backup approver. This will be on by default for every account.

We're making this change because many of you told us about invoices that sat unapproved for days while the assigned approver was out and nobody else was prompted to act. With escalation, a second person gets the reminder before the invoice goes overdue.

[NEED: how backup approvers are assigned, and what happens on approval steps that don't have one]

If 48 hours is too short or too long for your team, you can set a different window in Settings > Approvals > Escalation. You can also turn escalation off there if your approvers already cover for each other.

If you have questions about how this will affect your approval flows, reply to this email and it will come to us.
