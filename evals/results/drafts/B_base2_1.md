Subject: Starting March 1, unapproved invoices will escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi,

Starting March 1, 2027, invoice approval reminders in Ledgerline will escalate to a backup approver if an invoice goes unapproved for 48 hours. This will be on by default for all accounts.

**Why we're making this change**

Many of you told us that invoices get stuck when an approver is out of the office, traveling, or on leave. Payments get delayed, vendors follow up, and your team ends up chasing approvals by hand. Automatic escalation keeps invoices moving when the primary approver can't respond.

**What you can control**

You decide how escalation works for your organization. In **Settings > Approvals > Escalation**, you can:

- Change the escalation window to something shorter or longer than 48 hours
- Turn escalation off entirely

If the 48-hour default works for your team, you don't need to do anything.

**Before March 1**

We recommend reviewing your approval setup with your team so everyone knows who will receive escalated invoices.
