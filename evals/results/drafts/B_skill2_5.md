Subject: Starting March 1, unapproved invoices will escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi [First name],

Starting March 1, if an approver doesn't act on an invoice approval reminder within 48 hours, Ledgerline will send the invoice to their backup approver. Escalation will be on by default for every account.

You told us that invoices sit unapproved while approvers are out, and that someone has to notice the delay and chase it. With escalation, the invoice goes to someone who can act on it.

You can change this in Settings > Approvals > Escalation. You can set a shorter or longer window than 48 hours, or turn escalation off if your team already handles coverage another way. Nothing changes until March 1, so you have time to decide which setting fits.

Before then, check that each of your approvers has a backup assigned. [NEED: what happens to an invoice when its approver has no backup set, and where admins assign backups.]
