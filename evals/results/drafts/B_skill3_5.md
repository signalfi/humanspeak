Subject: Invoice approvals will escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours, starting March 1

Hi [First name],

Starting March 1, if an invoice approval reminder goes unanswered for 48 hours, Ledgerline will send the invoice to a backup approver. Escalation will be on by default for all accounts.

Customers told us that invoices sit unapproved when the assigned approver is out, and often nobody else knows they're waiting. With escalation, those invoices go to someone who can approve them instead of waiting for the approver to get back.

If 48 hours doesn't suit your team, you can change the window or turn escalation off in Settings > Approvals > Escalation.
