Subject: From March 1, stalled invoice approvals will escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi [First name],

Starting March 1, if an invoice approval reminder goes unanswered for 48 hours, Ledgerline will send the invoice on to a backup approver. Escalation will be on by default in every account.

You can change the 48-hour window or turn escalation off in Settings > Approvals > Escalation.

We're making this change because many of you told us invoices sit unapproved while the approver is on vacation, out sick or traveling, and nobody else can move them. Reminders don't help when the person getting them isn't there.
