Subject: From March 1, unapproved invoices escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi [First name],

Starting March 1, if an invoice approval reminder goes unanswered for 48 hours, Ledgerline will send the invoice to a backup approver. Escalation will be on by default for every account.

We're making this change because many of you told us invoices sit unapproved while the approver is out. Until now, an invoice waiting on someone who's away had nowhere else to go. With escalation, a second person gets it.

You can make the window shorter or longer than 48 hours, or turn escalation off, in Settings > Approvals > Escalation. If your team already has its own way of covering for absent approvers, or 48 hours doesn't match how quickly you approve, change the setting before March 1 so the default never applies to you.
