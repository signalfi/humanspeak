Subject: From March 1, unapproved invoices will escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi,

From March 1, if an approver doesn't act on an invoice approval reminder within 48 hours, Ledgerline will send the invoice to a backup approver. This will be on by default for all accounts.

Many of you told us that invoices sit unapproved while approvers are out, and that nobody finds out until the invoice is already late. Escalation gives each approval a second person to go to when the first one is away.

[NEED: how the backup approver is chosen, and what happens to an invoice when no backup is assigned]

You can change how long reminders wait before escalating, or turn escalation off, in Settings > Approvals > Escalation. If your approvers already cover for each other, or 48 hours is too short for how your team works, you might want to change the setting before March 1.
