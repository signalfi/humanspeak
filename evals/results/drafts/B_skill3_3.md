Subject: Starting March 1, unapproved invoices escalate to a backup approver after 48 hours

Hi,

Starting March 1, if nobody acts on an invoice approval reminder within 48 hours, Ledgerline will send that invoice to a backup approver. This will be on by default for every account.

You can change the 48-hour window, or turn escalation off completely, in Settings > Approvals > Escalation. If you don't change anything, the 48-hour default starts on March 1.

We're making this change because many of you told us about invoices that sat for days while the approver was on vacation or out sick, and the vendor kept waiting to be paid. Reminders don't help if the person getting them isn't there to read them.
