Subject: Starting March 1, overdue invoice approvals will escalate to a backup approver

Hi,

Starting March 1, if an invoice approval reminder goes unanswered for 48 hours, Ledgerline will send it to the approver's designated backup. This will be on by default for all accounts.

**Why we're making this change**

Many of you told us that invoices sit unapproved when an approver is out of office, traveling, or busy. That delays payments and creates work for your team. Escalation keeps approvals moving without anyone having to notice the delay and chase it down.

**What you can control**

You can adjust this in **Settings > Approvals > Escalation**:

- **Change the window.** Pick a shorter or longer period than 48 hours.
- **Turn escalation off.** Reminders will keep going only to the original approver.

**Before March 1**

We recommend checking that your approvers have the right backups assigned, so escalated invoices reach the people you intend.
